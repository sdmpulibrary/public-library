Join Us

Membership & borrowing · ಸದಸ್ಯತ್ವ

All students and staff of S.D.M. P.U. College can join the library. The library card is renewed every academic year.

💳

Who can join

  • Students of I and II PUC, on admission
  • Teaching and non-teaching staff (retired staff too)
  • Others, with the Librarian’s permission
📚

Borrowing

  • Students: 3 books for 15 days
  • Student Aid Fund (SAF) students: 3 more books
  • Staff: 12 books for 6 months
  • Others: 2 books for 15 days
  • Fine: ₹2 per day after the due date
  • Reference and “Not for loan” books are read in the library only
🕰️

Hours

9:00 AM – 5:30 PM

📝

How to Apply

  1. Fill the online membership form with your passport-size photo (sign in with your Google account to upload the photo).
  2. The library checks it and gives your Library ID. You get an e-mail.
  3. Collect your library ID card and borrower card at the counter.

Who We Are

About Us

The Advocate Shri V. G. Hegde Kaikatgeri Memorial Library and Information Centre is the library of M.P.E. Society’s S.D.M. P.U. College, Honnavar (U.K.). It is named after Advocate Shri V. G. Hegde Kaikatgeri.

The library serves the students and staff of I PUC and II PUC: Science, Commerce and Arts. Members can borrow books, read newspapers and magazines, use reference books, and find old question papers and e-resources on this website.

Open 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Closed on government holidays.
